# Security Policy

The Silkroad Developer Community (SDC) takes security seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose vulnerabilities to help keep our projects and community safe.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you've discovered a potential security vulnerability in any SDC project, please do **not** open a public issue. Instead, report it privately through one of the following channels:

- **Email**: [egezenn@tutanota.com](mailto:egezenn@tutanota.com)
- **Discord**: Contact a Maintainer (e.g., `@egezenn`) via the [SDC Discord Server](https://discord.gg/FEmNcz7QwP).

Please include as much information as possible to help us reproduce and address the issue:

- A description of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce.
- Potential impact.
- Any suggested fixes (if applicable).

## Disclosure Policy

We follow a responsible disclosure policy. Once you've reported a vulnerability, we will:

1. Acknowledge your report as soon as possible.
2. Investigate and work on a fix.
3. Keep you informed of our progress.
4. Publicly disclose the vulnerability once a fix is available (and give you credit if you wish).

Thank you for helping us maintain a secure environment!
